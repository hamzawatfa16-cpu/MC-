MY CONTENT — COOKIE NOTICE

Effective date: 11 September 2026

1. What this notice covers
This notice explains how the current My Content Windows desktop application handles cookies and similar browser storage during authentication and external web flows.

2. My Content is a native desktop application
My Content does not use a normal web page as its main interface and does not intentionally place advertising or tracking cookies on the application itself.

3. Google and Supabase authentication
The current sign-in flow uses Google through Supabase Auth. The authentication flow opens your normal web browser. Google, Supabase, and the browser may use cookies or similar technologies to keep the authentication flow working, protect accounts, remember preferences, or detect abuse. Those technologies are controlled by the relevant service and are governed by its own policies.

4. What My Content controls
My Content does not intentionally use browser cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking. The application uses the authentication session returned by Supabase after the Google sign-in flow is completed.

5. External websites
When My Content opens a third-party website, that website may use cookies or similar technologies according to its own privacy and cookie policies. My Content does not control those technologies.

6. Changes
This notice will be updated before the application intentionally introduces a new direct cookie or tracking use that materially changes the behavior described here.
